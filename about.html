About

The part that still needs a human.

There are faster tools now, and plenty of ways to create a design, build a website, shape a visual, or produce a piece of content. But tools don't always know when enough is enough. Too much colour becomes noise. Too much text gets ignored. A recipe has the same problem. It can list the salt, but it cannot tell you when the salt is right.

I currently work as an Operations Assistant at Professors Without Borders, handling end-to-end digital operations: annual reports, one-pagers, organisational documents, Monday.com automations, and social media strategy for an internationally distributed team.

Before that I produced live host-led shows as a Jr. Producer at Live Play Mobile, and ran an independent web and design service: building WordPress sites, handling hosting and databases, and designing social media for local clients. Earlier still I managed e-commerce for school IT procurement as E-Commerce Manager at Maytech Digital Provider.

Outside client work I founded InstaCilacap, a community platform for my region, and grew it from zero to 13.4K followers, though I no longer run it. I have released original music on Spotify and directed a short animated film made with the help of generative AI. The through line across all of it: taking something messy and making it clear, consistent, and worth looking at.
