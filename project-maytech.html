What the job covered

From the catalogue to the completed sale

Client budgets. I handled client procurement budgets of up to IDR 1 billion, covering hardware, software and chemistry laboratory equipment: sourcing, pricing, margin and catalogue, through to the completed sale.

Selling to schools. Maytech supplied IT equipment to schools through SIPLah, the government platform schools use to buy with their operational funds.

The store and everything around it. I managed the online store end to end, from listings, pricing, product photography and store presentation to the company website, hosting, orders and communication with buyers.

The store

Every listing, still live

I built every one of the store's 28 listings, and they are still live on SIPLah, where the store is rated 5/5. Together they cover what a school buys:

Classrooms and halls. 55 and 65-inch smart and interactive TVs, wireless microphones, surround speakers and HDMI switching.

Computers. All-in-one PCs, 2-in-1 laptops, desktop builds and keyboards.

School systems. A school management information system, a digital library system, CCTV, face and fingerprint attendance scanners, RFID cards and an ID card printer.

Furniture. Steel lockers and office desks.
