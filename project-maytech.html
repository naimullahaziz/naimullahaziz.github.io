What the job covered

From the catalogue to the completed sale

Client budgets. I handled client procurement budgets of up to IDR 1 billion, covering hardware, software and chemistry laboratory equipment: sourcing, pricing, margin and catalogue, through to the completed sale.

Selling to schools. Maytech supplied IT equipment to schools through SIPLah, the government platform schools use to buy with their operational funds.

The store and everything around it. I managed the online store end to end, from listings, pricing, product photography and store presentation to the company website, hosting, orders and communication with buyers.

Every order also went into a running order history.

Three stages. I ran each order through its full cycle and filed it in three stages: pre-order, order in progress and post-order, with a running history of every order.

The paperwork that goes with selling to schools. Work plans, work orders (SPK), invoices, proof of payment and the signed handover certificate (BAST) that closes the delivery.

The client's side too. I helped each school put together the full set of supporting documents its purchase required, including the tax payment receipts.

Reporting afterwards. Documentation, report preparation and video reports.

The storefront

How I set up the store

A store with its own identity

A banner and logo in Maytech blue, with the line Semudah yang kamu inginkan, “as easy as you want”, so the store reads as one company from the first screen.

Every photo carries the brand

Each product photo has the Maytech badge in the same corner, so a listing is recognisable in search results and on every product page.

Covers for systems, not stock photos

Software and service packages, such as the school management system, the digital library and CCTV, have their own designed covers with the product name set large.

All 28 listings

I built all 28 listings, covering what a school buys: classroom displays and audio, computers, school systems such as management, library and attendance, and furniture.
