Case study 04

Live production

Host-led shows where nothing can be fixed afterwards

Role
Jr. Producer
Organisation
Live Play Mobile Inc.
Location
San Francisco, remote
When
Jul to Oct 2025
Overview

Live host-led shows leave no room to fix things afterwards. I prepared scripts and content, briefed hosts, coordinated pre-show and real-time operations, watched the technical side and solved problems while the show was running, working with content and design teams to bring visuals and interactive elements into each broadcast.

What I was responsible for

  • Scripting and show content preparation
  • Host briefing and live coordination
  • Real-time monitoring and troubleshooting
  • Working across content and design teams
