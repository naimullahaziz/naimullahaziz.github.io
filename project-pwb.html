Case study 01

Professors Without Borders

Operations and communications for an international education non-profit

Role
Operations Assistant, previously Communication Assistant
Organisation
Professors Without Borders
Location
London, remote
When
Dec 2024 to present
Overview

Professors Without Borders is an international education non-profit that connects university educators with students in underserved communities. I started on communications and moved into operations, which now covers the website, digital materials and the running of programmes alongside colleagues in several countries.

What I was responsible for

  • Website content and programme pages
  • LinkedIn and social media, from planning to publishing
  • Visual materials for programmes and campaigns
  • Programme documentation and online sessions
  • Day-to-day operational support across projects
  • Coordination with colleagues across roles and time zones
Why this one matters

It is the clearest example of the combination I am actually good at: someone who can hold the operational thread of a programme and also build and publish the thing that communicates it, without needing two separate people.
