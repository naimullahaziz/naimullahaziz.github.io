Case study 02

Websites, hosting and online stores

Ten years of building and running sites for schools, companies and local clients

Role
Independent and in-house
Organisation
Vaeleon, Maytech and Muhammadiyah Vocational School
Location
Central Java, Indonesia
When
2015 to 2025
Overview

Web work has run through everything I have done, long before it was anyone's job title for me. I built and ran a vocational school's website and digital channels from nothing, supplied IT equipment to schools through Indonesia's government procurement platform while managing the store and the company site, and ran an independent web and design service for local clients.

What I was responsible for

  • WordPress builds, themes and plugin configuration
  • Hosting, domains, cPanel and database upkeep
  • Product listings, pricing and store presentation
  • Brand and graphic materials to match each site
  • Client brief through to launch and maintenance
  • School website and channels handed over still running
