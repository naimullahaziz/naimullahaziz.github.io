Education & IT · 2015–2023

Eight years,
one school.

IT and curriculum at a Muhammadiyah vocational high school in Central Java, with 60 teaching staff and several hundred students. Alongside the teaching, I helped build the school's branding, and built its website, YouTube channel and Instagram from zero before handing all three to staff.

What the job covered

Teaching, training and the school's channels

The IT curriculum. I held IT and curriculum for the school, planning the IT curriculum and coordinating it across year groups.

Bringing people up to date. I helped teaching staff and students keep up with the digital systems and tools in current use, and was regularly invited to speak as a subject resource.

The school's digital presence. When I started there was no website, no YouTube channel and no Instagram account. I built all three from zero, ran them, and handed every channel over to staff.

School branding

Helping shape the school's brand

I helped build the school's branding, and led the team that documented it in the 2023 profile book, 59 pages presenting the school in that branding.

The book carries the brand through every part of the school: curriculum, including the move to project-based learning from 2021; student affairs and extracurricular activities; facilities; public relations and partnerships with around 200 businesses and industry partners; and a section for each skill programme, from automotive engineering and pharmacy technology to accounting, Islamic banking and chemical analysis.

Eight parts of the book, 23 of its 59 pages, with the branding in use. Pick any one to page through it.
