Education & IT · 2015–2023

Eight years,
one school.

IT and curriculum at a Muhammadiyah vocational high school in Central Java, with 60 teaching staff and several hundred students. Alongside the teaching, I built the school's website, YouTube channel and Instagram from zero, then handed all three to staff.

What the job covered

Teaching, training and the school's channels

The IT curriculum. I held IT and curriculum for the school, planning the IT curriculum and coordinating it across year groups.

Bringing people up to date. I helped teaching staff and students keep up with the digital systems and tools in current use, and was regularly invited to speak as a subject resource.

The school's digital presence. When I started there was no website, no YouTube channel and no Instagram account. I built all three from zero, ran them, and handed every channel over to staff.

The profile book

The whole school in 56 pages

I led the team that put together the school's 2023 profile book, a 56-page publication presenting the school.

It covers every part of the school: curriculum, including the move to project-based learning from 2021; student affairs and extracurricular activities; facilities; public relations and partnerships with around 200 businesses and industry partners; and a section for each skill programme, from automotive engineering and pharmacy technology to accounting, Islamic banking and chemical analysis.
